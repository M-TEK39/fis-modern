User Manual for Auctions Section on the Fleet Information System

 

Purpose of Program

The Purpose of the Auctions program lies within the need for a uniform program for data capturing of accidents that will be and the subsequent analysis of the outcome of the matters.

Term/ Field Analysis

The detail of the required data fields are in most cases self-explanatory but will however be specified.

 

It is the assumption that each and every user of the GGMT Administrative functions on the Fleet Information System has received on the job training in the specified field that pertains to his/ her division.

 

Auction Maintenance

The capturing/ updating of data for vehicles that has been involved in accidents.

Delete a Vehicle on Auction

Should a vehicle be found not to be compulsory to withdraw this function will delete the identified vehicle and the auction related information from the data base .

Auction Number

Identification of the date on which the specified vehicle is proposed to be auctioned.

Camp

Specification of the Auction Camp where the vehicle will be stationary during the proposed auction.

Lot number

Unique Number allocated to the vehicle. Usually this will also indicated the sequence in which the vehicles will be auctioned. The lowest numbers first. This field will be completed when the auction vehicles have been confirmed after the board of surveys.

Bar Code

The identification of the Bar Coding on the vehicles as a secondary identification system.

Auth. Number

The authority number given by the Board Of Surveys to dispose of the specified vehicle.

Auth Date

Date board of surveys convened.

Km

Odometer reading on the proposed withdrawn vehicle.

Sold Reason

Combo box with options indication the reason of withdrawal eg. Uneconomical to repair.

Estimated Price

Price estimated by the specialist according to the Mead McGrather.

Reserve Price

The lowest possible prices that will be accepted on the day of the Auction.

Sold Price

The Price the vehicle was sold for on the day of the auction.

Sold To Name

Person the vehicle sold to.

Sold To ID

ID number of the person the vehicle was sold to

Sold Date

Date vehicle was sold.

Remarks

Additional information relevant to the vehicle.
