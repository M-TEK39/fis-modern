User Manual for Fuel Card Section on the Fleet Information System

 

Purpose of Program

The Purpose of the Fuel cards program lies within the need for a uniform program for data capturing related to the fuel cards and the subsequent analysis of the outcome of the matters.

 

Term/ Field Analysis

The detail of the required data fields are in most cases self-explanatory but will however be specified.

 

It is the assumption that each and every user of the GGMT Administrative functions on the Fleet Information System has received on the job training in the specified field that pertains to his/ her division. Fields that is seen on more than one page will not be duplicated in the Term/ Field analysis, as the meanings/ definitions would stay within similar parameters.

 

Print Page

Indicate whether or not the page needs to be printed or not.

Garage

Identify the GMT depot the vehicles is from

GG Counter

System Generated number of cards issued by GGMT

Bank Counter

Number of cards issued by the bank this financial year

Card Number

The registration number appearing on the card.

Pan Number

The card personal account number as indicated on the card.

Link GG Number

The GG number linked to this card in instances where the card has been issued on a provincial number.

Receiver

The name of the person that collected this specific card

Id Number

ID number of the person that collected the fuel card.

REC Site

The specified departmental site of the person that collected the fuel card.

Tel Number

Contact telephone number of the person that collected the card

Fax Number

The fax number of the collector ( person who collected the card)

Receive Bank

The date the card has been received from the bank

Taken date

Date the card was collected by the user.

Expiry Date

Date the card will expire

Replace Reason

Identify the reason for replacement

Date of Incident

Date of the incident that caused that a new card t be ordered.

Comment

Essential information that must be taken into consideration

Submit

Submits the information to the data base.
