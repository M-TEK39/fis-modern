GOVERNMENT CHAUFFEUR DRIVEN SERVICES

PROCEDURES MANUAL

 

1. FOREWORD
This Government Garage service is available in the following two instances:
1.1 When government officials, on official business, are flying to Johannesburg International Airport and need to be chauffeur driven to any other destination in and around Gauteng.
1.2 When government officials, on official business, need to be chauffeur driven to and from any destination in South Africa.

2. PROCEDURE FOR MAKING BOOKING
2.1 Bookings are to be made a minimum of 12 hours before the expected pickup time.
2.2 Make use of the trip request booking form and complete every part in the full.
2.3 Fax the form to the VIP office you want to make the booking with, which will be either our Pretoria VIP or Johannesburg VIP office. DO NOT place bookings with both offices as it will be considered a double booking and your department will be liable for both booking costs.
2.4 Confirm the fax with a phone call to the relevant office to ensure that the fax was received and that the information on the request is complete (we will not be responsible for bookings not received due to fax error, therefore you have to confirm receipt of your fax by our VIP offices).

3. ESSENTIAL AND IMPORTANT INFORMATION
All trips have to be pre-approved by the responsible financial officer and / or transport manager who has to ensure that the necessary funds are available to pay for the booking. All information needed on the trip request must be completed in full, especially where a flight arrival (number) is concerned, otherwise the trip will be declined by the Government Garage. No person will be chauffeur driven before the necessary paperwork (e.g.. trip request form) have been received by our VIP offices.

4. CANCELLATIONS
Bookings are to be cancelled in writing by fax and at least a minimum of two hours before the pre-booked pickup time. In the event that this does not happen, the user department who made the booking will be liable for all costs incurred by the Government Garage.

5. NO SHOW BOOKINGS
In the event that a booking was made and the official to be picked up cannot be found at the pre-booked pickup point, the trip will be considered a "NO SHOW" on the user department`s side and all costs incurred by the Government Garage will be claimed against the user department. With officials flying to Johannesburg Airport, our Airport Kiosk will trace any early, delayed and / or cancelled flights so as to manage the pickup times for the relevant flight numbers as stipulated on the trip request form. Where officials miss or change their flights, our VIP offices need to be informed of such changes immediately, otherwise the trip will be considered as a no show and your department will be liable for costs incurred for the relevant booking.
