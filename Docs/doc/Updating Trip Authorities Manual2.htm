Procedure Manual Related To The Updating Of Odometer On The ELS.

To be read in conjunction with the Electronic Logsheet and Trip Authority Manual available on the ELS.

 

Purpose of Procedure

The purpose of the updating of odometer on the ELS lies in the possibility that the ELS/ FIS may be unavailable for some or other reason that includes but is not restricted to power failures, network unavailability and/ or logistical difficulties.

 

It however needs to be emphasized that the updating of odometer should only be used to supplement the daily use of the Trip Authority issuing in extreme circumstances where all other logistical possibilities has been exhausted. By no means may the updating of odometer readings be used as primary capturing method as this will have a definite impact on the integrity of the usage data and subsequently nullify the objective of driver and usage control of the ELS as a management and reporting tool.

 

Subsequent to all the above GGMT would like to take the time to confirm that offices should refrain from using the consent to update odometer reading as an authority to abolish the daily usage of the Electronic Logsheet System and/ or disregard of prescribed and effective office practice.

 

What does updating entail?

Updating of odometer readings is necessitated by any circumstance where the odometer on the ELS does not reflect the current odometer reading in the vehicle due to trips that has not been captured yet being for a day, week or month.

 

This is not a method to rectify incorrectly captured odometer readings as incorrect information should be referred to the Call Centre for rectification at number (011)372-8600.

 

Financial Implication

The updating of odometer readings must be done in adherence to the budget allocation for the specified routes, trips, journeys.

 

By implication it is necessary to group the sequential kilometers for a specific budget allocation together and repeat the process for all other routes, trips, journeys for other budget allocations.

 

Kindly note that if updating is done for a time-span that exceeds the history of the current financial year (The odometer reading of the vehicle is reflecting the reading for the previous financial year) the funds will have to be taken from the current budget.

 

It needs to be emphasized that updating may result in reporting that do not reflect the vehicle utilization by a driver and/ or the areas traveled perfectly.

 

Ho Do I Update?

 

The process of updating can be adapted according to the specific circumstances of the office. There are three basic updating methods that will be discussed with its pros and cons.

 

The basic principles of all the methods will be similar; it is only in the interpretation of routes that there is a difference.

 

In the updating process the only nominal differences from a standard Trip Authority lies in the fields for Reason for trip, the route information and the date specification.

Principle 2

The departure and arrival location (Arrow B) should be specified upon the routes per specific budget. A route could also for updating purposes be identified as an operational area for example Gauteng and Gauteng Area.

 

Note 1

It could be said that one route should be for one budget. Meaning that if you combine 2 or more routes for the purpose of updating they could be combined under one route budget wise.

 

Example: Route 1 has been traveled under objective X and responsibility Y; Route 2 has been traveled under objective X and responsibility Y; route three has been traveled under objective X and responsibility Y.

 

One route can be made to combine the three routes into 1 provided that the routes were undertaken in sequence (Kilometer order) and no other budget allocation was used in between the routes.

 

Principle 3

Arrow C indicates the allocated space for the capturing of the estimated distance. For the purpose of updates the user could capture the actual distance traveled.

 

Principle 4

Arrow D and E indicate the allocated space for the specification of the date. For the purpose of updating the start and end date should reflect the day the update is done. The system cannot backdate.

 

Principle 5

Arrows F, G, H indicates the combo block from which the correct Responsibility, Objective and Project description respectively.

 

Method 1

 

Update one electronic trip authority for one manual trip authority.

 

Please note to attach the documentation to the updated trip authority as proof.

 

 

The pro in this updating method lies in the conservation of data integrity.

 

The con in this method is the fact that this method tend to be time consuming especially if many days or months updates need to be done.

 

Method 2

 

Update one trip authority for one month or for one logsheet.

 

Please note to attach the documentation to the updated trip authority as proof.

 

The pro in this method lies in the capability to capture many days worth of kilometers on 1 trip authority. The one route per budget still applies to this method.

 

The con in this method lie in the loss of data integrity related to drivers.

 

 

 

Method 3

 

Multiple trip authorities updated on one trip authority with multiple routes.

 

Please note to attach the documentation to the updated trip authority as proof.

 

The pro in this method lies in the capability to capture many days worth of kilometers on 1 trip authority. The one route per budget still applies to this method.

 

The con in this method lie in the loss of data integrity related to drivers.

 

 

 

Conclusion

 

The updating should be done in accordance with the prescribed guidelines to enable the auditors to identify the reasons for high distances per day or month.

 

All proof documents should be attached to the updated trip authority for verification.

 

The identification of the date the updating is done for on the reason for trip field is essential.

 

 

 

Kindly note that the Electronic Logsheet Training Manual is available online on website http://gfleet.pwv.gov.za the homepage option number 2.

 

Essential information:

 

All update authorities must be accompanied by all the relevant source documents.

 

All update authorities must be signed by the delegated signatory in adherence to the regulatory prescriptions for all trip authorities.

 

No trip authority’s odo span may exceed 25000km as the system will not accept the span.

 

Should a trip authority exceed such km it must be split into two.
